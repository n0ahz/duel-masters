Duel Masters

Complete Game Rules

ISetup

Basic Setup

Both players perform these activities to set up the beginning of a game:

The following are basic rules and must be followed.

  • Shuffle your deck.
  • Take 5 cards from the top of your deck without looking at them and put them in a row in front of you face down. These face-down cards are your shields.
  • Draw 5 cards from the top of your deck.
  • Decide who goes first by a randomly decided process.
    • This can be done as a coin toss, a dice roll, or via Rock-Paper-Scissors.

Official Gameplay Mat

Advanced Setup

Complete these steps if you have these card types in your deck:

  • Psychic / Dragheart
    • Separate all Psychic Creatures and Draghearts into the Hyperspatial Zone.
  • Forbidden
    • Put all Forbidden Impulses or Final Forbidden Fields into the zone dictated by the card. If placed in the battle zone, put the required number of seals on it.
  • Gacharange
    • Put all Gacharange Creatures into the Super Gacharange Zone. You must have exactly 0 or 12 cards. It is shuffled at the start of the game.

IIHow to Win

Attack your opponent with your creatures. Each time one of your creatures attacks and isn't blocked, it breaks one or more of the opponent's shields.

You win the game when you attack your opponent with a creature that isn't blocked when they have no shields left.

You can also win when your opponent has no cards left in their deck or would draw their last card.

Some cards will enable you to win via their effects.

IIITurn Order

  1. 1

    Start Your Turn

    If you have no tapped cards, nothing happens. If you have any tapped cards, untap them now. Then, effects that trigger at the start of the turn fire.

  2. 2

    Draw a Card

    Only the person who plays first skips drawing on their first turn. As soon as you draw the last card of your deck, you lose.

  3. 3

    Put a Card into Your Mana Zone

    You can put only 1 card (of any type) into your mana zone each turn, unless an effect allows more. Put it upside down so you can read its mana number.

  4. 4

    Play Cards

    Play as many creatures, spells, cross gear, castles, and auras as your mana zone can afford, in any order.

  5. 5

    Attack with Your Creatures

    Attack with creatures in the battle zone. You can't attack with creatures you just summoned — they have summoning sickness. As many of your creatures as you want may attack each turn.

  6. 6

    End Your Turn

    Tell your opponent you are finished. Now it's their turn. Continue until one of you wins.

IVHow to Use the Card Types

How to cast a Spell

Choose a spell from your hand. The cost is in the upper left corner. Pay by tapping cards in your mana zone — at least 1 must match the spell's civilization. After paying, resolve its effect, then put it into your graveyard.

How to summon a Creature

Choose a creature from your hand. Pay its cost by tapping mana (at least 1 matching civilization). Put it into the battle zone. Creatures have Summoning Sickness — they can attack on your next turn.

How to summon an Evolution Creature

An evolution creature is summoned like a regular creature, but you need the correct creature already in the battle zone. Place it on top of the creature it evolves from — only the evolution creature's stats count. Evolution creatures don't get summoning sickness. When moved from the battle zone, the whole pile moves with it.

How to generate or cross a Cross Gear

A Cross Gear is generated by paying its cost. It remains in the battle zone until crossed. To cross it onto a creature, pay its cost again and choose the target. A crossed cross gear can be transferred by paying its cost again. Shield Trigger Cross lets you generate and cross for free when a shield breaks.

How to fortify a Castle

A Castle is attached directly to a shield. It activates its ability while the shield remains in the shield zone — some protect the shield from breaking in exchange for a different shield.

How to use a Psychic Creature

Psychic Creatures are placed in your Hyperspatial Zone at game start. They enter the battle zone only via a card effect (e.g. a Hyperspatial spell). When they would leave the battle zone, they return to the Hyperspatial Zone instead. Some feature Psychic Link — at the start of your turn, you can link two or more Psychic Creatures to awaken them into a Psychic Super Creature.

How to use a Dragheart card

Draghearts are placed in your Hyperspatial Zone and enter the battle zone via a Draguner card's effect. There are three types: Dragheart Weapon (equipped to the draguner), Dragheart Fortress (placed horizontally, independent), and Dragheart Creature (produced via Dragsolution). A Dragheart Creature with Dragon Evade can flip back to its Fortress side when it would leave the battle zone.

How to play a Forbidden card

A Forbidden card starts as a Forbidden Impulse. Once in play, place seals (cards from the top of your deck) on it. When you play a Command of the same civilization, remove a seal. When all seals are gone, it Forbidden Liberates into a Forbidden Creature. A Forbidden card counts toward your 40-card deck.

Final Forbidden Field

Put into the battle zone at game start with a set number of seals. Each seal removal triggers an effect. When the final seal is removed, it Forbidden Big Bangs into a Final Forbidden Creature (5 cards). These impose limits on your creatures and control how seals are removed.

How to play a D2 Field

A D2 Field is a horizontal card that enters the battle zone by tapping mana, triggering an effect (usually buffing your creatures). Some have Denjara Switch — flip it upside down for a one-time effect. Only one D2 Field can exist in the battle zone at a time; placing a new one sends the old one to its owner's graveyard.

How to play a Neo Creature

A NEO Creature can be played as a regular creature or placed on top of another creature to be treated as a Neo Evolution Creature. If evolved, it does not get summoning sickness and is affected by evolution creature effects.

How to play a Twinpact

Twinpact cards have two halves — a creature and a spell — each with its own cost. You may choose to summon the creature half or cast the spell half. You cannot do both simultaneously.

How to play a Gacharange Creature

Gacharange Creatures begin in your Super Gacharange Zone (12 cards, max 2 copies of any card). To summon one, Gacharange Summon: reveal the top card of the zone and place it in the battle zone — no mana is tapped.

How to play an Orega Aura

Play an Orega Aura by attaching it to one of your Gacharange Creatures in the battle zone, or Gacharange Summon first and attach to the summoned creature. Auras are stacked sideways in a T-shape; their power and abilities are added to the base creature.

VAttacking Your Opponent

To attack, tap one of your creatures and declare what it's attacking — your opponent directly, or one of their tapped creatures. No additional mana is paid.

When a creature attacks directly and isn't blocked, it breaks one of your opponent's shields. Your opponent picks up that shield and puts it in their hand.

If multiple shields break in a single attack, they break simultaneously. The opponent may then use any Shield Trigger cards in any order. Cards with Strike Back must wait until the next attack.

Creatures with a Breaker ability break more shields per attack. If the opponent has fewer shields than the creature can break, only the remaining shields are broken.

Attacking a Tapped Creature

When your creature attacks a tapped opponent's creature and isn't blocked, the creatures battle. The creature with higher power wins. The loser is put into its owner's graveyard. If powers are equal, both are destroyed.

Effects When Attacking

After tapping to attack: resolve "whenever this creature attacks" effects, then your opponent may choose a blocker. If no block, apply remaining creature effects, then break shields (or win if no shields remain).

Blocking an Attack

Only creatures with the Blocker ability can block. The blocker must be untapped (summoning sickness does not prevent blocking). When a creature blocks, it battles the attacker — the higher-power creature wins; equal power destroys both.

Order of Attack Steps

When two creatures battle: first follow any instructions on the attacking creature, then the blocking/defending creature. Then compare powers — lower power goes to the graveyard; equal power destroys both. Then any post-battle effects trigger.

⚑
Revolution 0 Trigger: When your opponent has no shields and you attack directly, they may declare a Revolution 0 Trigger. If the connect requirement is met, the effect fires and the attack is stopped. Multiple may be declared, but the first that fails to connect ends the game. All successive ones must each connect or the opponent loses.
